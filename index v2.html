Field Range Finder

Estimates distance with the phone camera and tilt sensor. Good for golf and range practice. Not a laser.

Accuracy depends on how well you bracket the target and on the size you enter. For hunting, use a laser rangefinder for any shot decision.

BASE

Bracket a target of the selected size from a distance you know, then tap Set. Do this once per phone. Don't zoom.
--
Hold the phone upright. Put the crosshair on the target's base on flat ground. To zero, hold it upright against a vertical wall and tap Zero tilt.
Estimate only. Confirm with a laser rangefinder before any hunting shot.
